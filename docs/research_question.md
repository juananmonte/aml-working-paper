# Research Question

Can Bayesian AML-state uncertainty and posterior surprise identify
which cases deserve local causal investigation, improving causal
mechanism recovery under a limited investigation budget?

## Primary hypothesis

Under a fixed investigation budget, Bayesian uncertainty-guided
routing improves causal mechanism recovery compared with random
and risk-only routing.

## Unit of analysis

An AML investigation candidate at time t.

## Main outcome

Causal mechanism recovery under investigation budget B.
